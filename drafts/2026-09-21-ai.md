## Post text (edit freely, this is what gets published)

Building AI agents that can handle complex evaluations without manual oversight is one of the biggest architectural hurdles we face today. When autonomous workflows require risk scoring and stakeholding checks, keeping them consistent is tough.

Salesforce has shared guidance on building a structured decision-making pipeline for agent skills, focusing on how to weave together decision recording, risk scoring, and stakeholder recommendations.

Picture a case where an agent evaluates a complex financial application and needs to run a structured scoring routine before suggesting a final path to a human reviewer. Rather than relying on unstructured prompts, the agent invokes a defined decision skill that calculates risk metrics and logs the rationale step by step.

For enterprise Salesforce work and Financial Services Cloud implementations, this matters because compliance and auditability are non-negotiable. Having a structured scoring pipeline ensures that automated agent actions leave a clear, reviewable trail for every recommendation they make.

How are you currently handling risk scoring and audit trails inside your automated agent workflows?
