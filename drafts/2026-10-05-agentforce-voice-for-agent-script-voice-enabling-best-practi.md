## Post text (edit freely, this is what gets published)

Building autonomous agents that sound natural over the phone is one of the trickiest design challenges we face right now. Text prompts just do not translate directly to spoken conversation.

Salesforce recently shared guidance on voice-enabling an Agent Script agent using Agentforce Voice, focusing on configuring voice settings and writing instructions tailored for the ear.

Picture a case where an advisor's client calls in to check a loan status, and the agent needs to read back complex financial figures without sounding robotic or confusing. The guidelines emphasize structuring instructions so spoken interactions remain clear and concise.

For Financial Services Cloud implementations, this matters immensely because compliance disclosures and account balances must be communicated accurately and naturally over voice channels without frustrating the caller.
