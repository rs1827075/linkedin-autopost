## Post text (edit freely, this is what gets published)

Catching bugs late in the release cycle usually means expensive rollbacks and frustrated stakeholders. We all need better ways to catch issues before code hits production environments.

Salesforce has made testing in DevOps Center generally available, helping teams automate tests and enforce quality gates earlier in the pipeline. This brings shift-left practices directly into native tooling.

Picture a case where your team implements automated Apex tests that must pass in a staging environment before any promotion is allowed to proceed. While specific configuration steps depend on your pipeline setup, the goal is stopping regressions before they impact end users.

For financial services implementations, this native enforcement is vital when handling strict compliance and automated data validations. Ensuring quality gates are met natively reduces the risk of deploying broken financial calculation logic or flawed triggers.
